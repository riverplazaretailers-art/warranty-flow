# Warranty Flow

Build a polished teaser/coming-soon website for Two River Ops (domain: TwoRiverOps.com), an AI-first operations company for equipment dealers. The first product is warranty administration: the system reads repair records, prepares claims, submits through OEM-authorized integrations, monitors status, resolves routine rejections, and reconciles payment, with humans only handling true exceptions. Do not oversell unapproved OEM relationships. Do not display Deere, Bobcat, CNH, Kubota, or other OEM logos or imply partnership/authorization. Tone: credible, restrained, industrial/enterprise, modern; think premium B2B infrastructure, not flashy AI startup. Visual direction: warm white / very light stone background, charcoal/navy typography, one restrained accent, generous whitespace, sharp typography, subtle industrial details, no robot/AI-brain imagery. Mobile responsive.

Create a single-page public teaser with these sections:
1) Minimal nav: Two River Ops wordmark left; 'Warranty' and 'About' anchor links; CTA 'Join the pilot'.
2) Hero: eyebrow 'AI OPERATIONS FOR EQUIPMENT DEALERS'; headline 'The warranty desk, without the warranty administrator.' Supporting copy: 'Two River Ops is building an AI-operated warranty service that turns repair records into complete claims, works routine exceptions, and tracks reimbursement—with people only where judgment is actually required.' CTAs: 'Join the pilot' and secondary 'How it works'. Include a subtle right-side workflow visualization rather than a dashboard screenshot.
3) Credibility strip with non-claiming phrases: 'Built for equipment dealers' · 'OEM-specific workflows' · 'Human-backed exceptions' · 'Secure dealer access'.
4) How it works: Repair record → Claim built → Submitted → Exceptions resolved → Payment tracked. Explain that routine work is automated and unresolved facts are escalated as one specific question.
5) Differentiation section headline 'Not another tool for your warranty administrator.' Two-column comparison: Traditional warranty admin tasks vs Two River Ops handles the workflow. Avoid claims that the role is always eliminated today; frame as the target operating model and pilot objective.
6) Product teaser cards: 'Managed Warranty' (ongoing administration from repair record through reimbursement) and 'Recovery' (historical denied/short-paid claim review as a fixed-fee service). Mark Managed Warranty as 'Pilot'.
7) Pilot section: target construction, agricultural, material-handling and commercial equipment dealers; one OEM / capped-volume pilot; dealer keeps control and answers only missing factual questions. CTA form capturing Name, Work email, Company, OEM brands represented, optional note. Make form submission show a polished success state; local-only is fine if no backend configured.
8) About section: 'Two River Ops builds AI-operated back-office workflows where software does the repetitive work and people handle exceptions.' Keep broad enough for future products beyond warranty.
9) Footer with Two River Ops, 'Equipment operations, rebuilt for AI.' and placeholder links Privacy / Terms (can point to # for now), plus '© 2026 Two River Ops'.

SEO/title: 'Two River Ops | AI Warranty Operations for Equipment Dealers'. Meta description focused on AI-operated warranty administration for equipment dealers. Add tasteful microinteractions, accessible contrast, and professional copy. No pricing on the teaser. No invented customer logos, testimonials, savings percentages, partner badges, or unsupported claims.
